## Roofline formula (tuning/roofline.py:461-504, confirmed)
fraction = achieved/ceiling. achieved = GEMM_flops_per_step / wall_per_step. flops via torch.profiler(with_flops, use_fused=False)=eager GEMMs (elementwise=0, conservative). wall = differenced CUDA-sync over the REAL megakernel (use_fused=True). ceiling = min(engine_peak, AI*HBM_BW); engine_peak follows the engine (wgmma->989TF, scalar->66.9TF). d flows from config; seq is per-model hardcoded.
